Teaching & Mentoring

I have taught research methods, quantitative analysis, social stratification, and the sociology of the family at undergraduate, master's, and doctoral levels in the United States and Sweden. At the University of Louisville I received the Faculty Favorite Teaching Award (2013–2014).
