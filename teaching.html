Teaching & Mentoring

As an instructor, I aim to help students learn to think scientifically, ask meaningful questions about the social world, weigh competing explanations, and draw on empirical evidence to inform arguments. I have taught and supervised students with very different levels of preparation, from first-generation undergraduates to doctoral students and postdocs, primarily in the United States, and to a lesser extent in Sweden. I want to create an inclusive learning environment in which all students feel comfortable participating and all develop skills that they can use beyond the classroom. At the University of Louisville, I received the Faculty Favorite Teaching Award for the academic year 2013–2014.
