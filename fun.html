Fun Things About Me

Life in two countries

I was born in the United States, studied and began my career there, and moved to Sweden in 2017, where I am now also a citizen. Living between two very different countries has its challenges. Stockholm is a beautiful city and the people are lovely, but the long winter darkness can be brutal. My remedy is to fill our apartment with warm, cozy lights, which brightens things up more than you might think! I also miss my life in the U.S., especially my family and friends and the many wonderful colleagues I have worked with over the years.

Family

I love spending time with my family, especially my son, who plays basketball, and my husband, who is an excellent cook (lucky me!). And no introduction to our household would be complete without Mika, our super friendly cat, who is always happy to greet anyone who comes through the door.

Outside work

I love being outdoors. I don’t know much about nature, but I love everything about it, and any excuse to be outside is a good one. I also play a little guitar, though I’m no expert!

Currently reading

I love to read. Right now I’m on the fourth book in the Millennium series, The Girl in the Spider’s Web by David Lagercrantz, which continues Stieg Larsson’s original trilogy. It’s a fitting choice for someone living in Stockholm, where much of the series takes place.
