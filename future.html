Future Plans

I plan to continue building an active, externally funded research program, publishing regularly, and mentoring students and early career researchers. In the coming years, my work will focus on:

  1. Work and health disparities. Examining how working conditions contribute to health disparities, in Sweden and the United States, and developing new projects for external funding in this area.
  2. Work, families, and children. Examining how work-related experiences extend beyond individual workers to affect families and children, bringing together my research on work and health with my earlier work on families and intergenerational processes.
  3. Working longer, and who can. Extending my research on working life expectancy to understand who is able to remain in paid work as populations age, and why.
  4. Strengthening the evidence. Using causal analytic methods with longitudinal survey and register data to better identify the effects of work on health, producing evidence that may help inform workplace and social policy.
